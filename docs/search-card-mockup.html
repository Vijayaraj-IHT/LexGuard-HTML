  • Wrong year. 2020 appeal rendered as 1961 — 178 of 434 rows whose case number ends in a year have a contradicting year field.
  • Fabricated "official" citation. 1961 LexGuard HC FEE6CF7B — 535 of 607 citations contain an internal “LexGuard” ID; they are not official citations.
  • Placeholder bench. “Hon'ble Bench” is stored as a judge name in 308 rows and gets a person icon.
  • 100% Match is meaningless. Empty/browse queries return relevance_score = 1.0, so every card claims 100%.
  • Empty green pill — low-contrast status badge carries no readable label.
  • No hierarchy. Eight pills compete; court, year, outcome and status all look equally important.
  • Gold text (#d97706) fails WCAG AA at 3.19 : 1 on white.
